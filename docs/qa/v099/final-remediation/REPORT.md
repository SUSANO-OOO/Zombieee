findings in the Version 0.9.9.0 final remediation scope. The authoritative raw

`outputs/v099-final-remediation-evidence-final/`; `EVIDENCE_INDEX.json` records every

## Acceptance results

1. Normal battle BGM uses the dedicated `music-v099-normal` asset. The tracked
   30-second normal, pressure, boss, and transition captures were generated by
   an offline reconstruction of the production gain, bus, crossfade, dialogue
   duck, and transient-duck graph. They are technical signal evidence, not a
   hardware-loopback or subjective listening claim.
2. The actual Stage 3 TAKUYA story-to-battle path kept scene `boss` and asset
   `music-v099-boss` through entrance and final story events, maintained one
   active BGM voice, applied and released the entrance duck, and returned to the
   then-current pressure scene after defeat. Chromium passed audio and logic in
   four cases. WebKit passed logic in four cases; its Playwright runtime did not
   expose `AudioContext`, which is retained as a capability gap rather than a
   product pass. Local-QA hydration diagnostics are retained separately; the
   fail-closed setup/runtime boundary begins only after the target Stage 3 asset
   generation is stable, and accepts no request failure inside that boundary.
3. Seven deployment families were checked at six keyframes across four browser
   axes (168 checkpoints). Every unit used alpha 1, one sprite draw, no reveal
   clip, no ghost duplicate, and authored vehicle foreground occlusion.
4. Mobile HUD evidence covered eight states across four browser axes. Required
   action text was at least 14 px, secondary text at least 12 px, with no
   accepted-zone, banner/bark, boss-semantic, or boss/crawler-alert overlap.
5. CRAWLER barrage and airstrike equipment use project-original, seven-state
   RGBA raster sheets with state-specific physical structure. Aligned-alpha
   silhouette tests reject pure translation and sub-pixel-only differences,
   require adjacent structural differences, and verify that firing/recoil and
   targeting/inbound/confirmation remain distinct after external VFX and HUD
   are excluded. All states remain distinguishable at the actual 844x390 and
   844x340 runtime scale. Across 56 captured phase screenshots and eight raw
   runtime contact sheets there was no idle floating equipment and no Canvas
   body geometry; input, cooldown, targeting, damage, trajectory, and transient
   action effects remained available.
