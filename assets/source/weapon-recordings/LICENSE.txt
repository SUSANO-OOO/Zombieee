Selected original near-field stereo recordings from the archive's master sheet:
D_32P.wav: AR-15 / M4, semi automatic, 5.56x45, individual shots.
C_28P.wav: AK-47, 7.62x39, individual shots.
G_31P.wav: Carl Gustav M45 / Swedish K, 9mm, individual shots.
X_39P.wav: Walther PPQ, striker handgun, 9mm, individual shots.
The game uses these as authored weapon families, not exact real-world model claims.
Runtime derivatives: selected distinct discharges, mono downmix, trimmed lead-in,
gentle frequency filtering, bounded transient-preserving levels, faded tails.
A/B comparison clips attenuate the prior recording to match active-signal RMS;
that numeric normalization is not a subjective listening-quality acceptance.
No pitch shift, synthetic shot or additional burst is introduced.
MP3 is the preferred mobile format; Ogg retains the existing browser fallback.
