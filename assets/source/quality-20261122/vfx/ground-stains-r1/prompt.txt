Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one four-cell transparent raster terrain-decal atlas for a gritty, painterly Japanese post-apocalyptic mobile strategy game. The stains will be composited over rainy cracked asphalt and dusty concrete in a side-on battle, at about 25–65 pixels in width.
Generate a 2×2 atlas, equally sized cells, with exactly one isolated dark burgundy ground stain per cell, centred in its own cell with generous fully transparent gutters. These are four different flat surface residues seen straight down from above, with the background completely transparent everywhere except the liquid and tiny spray droplets.
Top left: a small weathered reddish-black splash with an asymmetrical hooked rivulet and a few irregular pinhead specks.
Top right: a sideways sweeping taper with thin broken dark rivulets stretching in one direction and a dense middle.
Bottom left: a broader flattened irregular burgundy deposit where several patches have flowed together, with torn edges, small holes and subdued wet grain.
Bottom right: a tight scattered crimson-brown stain with several thin branching spatter trails fading away in different directions.
Natural hand-painted photorealistic material. Dark oxblood, warm charcoal and rusty brown edges, subtle low-contrast wet highlights and believable partial transparency. Screen silhouette should remain visibly organically irregular at thumbnail scale and read as a stain in the floor rather than a floating red plate. Avoid neon, bright red, sharp graphic rims, large glossy white specular spots, ovals, circles, uniform rims, frame borders, external cast shadows, colored backgrounds and completely solid interior fills. No flooring or pavement itself, people, bodies, flesh, objects, writing, label, watermark, or UI. Deliver with genuine alpha.
